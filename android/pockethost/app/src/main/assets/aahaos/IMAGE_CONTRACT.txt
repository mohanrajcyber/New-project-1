PocketHost ↔ AahaOS image contract (v1)
=======================================

Guest: AahaOS (custom embedded Linux). Not an ISO wizard. Not VMware.

On-device paths after the host copies a built image:

  files/aahaos/manifest.json
  files/aahaos/<arch>/vmlinuz
  files/aahaos/<arch>/initramfs.cpio.gz

<arch> is x86_64 (dev / QEMU on PC) or aarch64 (phones later).

manifest.json fields:
  os, version, arch, kind=embedded-linux
  not: windows, vmware, generic-iso, from-scratch-kernel

VmEngine reports Ready when this contract / bundled manifest is present.
Ready does NOT mean QEMU is executing. Start() may return Unavailable
until a real engine (Termux QEMU or JNI) is wired.

Build the image on a Linux box:  make image
Then copy images/x86_64/* next to this contract, or ship them as assets.
