PocketHost ↔ AahaOS image contract (v0.5)
========================================

Guest: AahaOS (custom embedded Linux). Not an ISO wizard. Not Kali.

Published phone images (public repo):

  dist/aarch64/vmlinuz
  dist/aarch64/core/initramfs.cpio.gz
  dist/aarch64/net/initramfs.cpio.gz
  dist/aarch64/lab/initramfs.cpio.gz
  dist/aarch64/study/initramfs.cpio.gz

Variants (ours only): core | net | lab | study
  core  = console guest, local-only
  net   = virtio-net + udhcpc + dropbear
  lab   = net + extra applets (hexdump/od/strace/tcpdump)
  study = lab + aaha study / aaha lesson 1-4 (authorized classroom use)

Persist: AAHA_DISK is virtio-blk mounted at /data as vfat (not ext2).
This virt kernel's netboot modules have fat/vfat, not ext2/ext4.

Study tools are for systems you own or have written permission to test.
No exploit kits. No attack payloads. Not a Kali clone.

Ready does NOT mean QEMU is executing inside the APK.
Phone boot: docs/TERMUX.md
